Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join?

Create a free account, choose a plan, pick a coach (optional) and pay online. Your membership starts right away.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. Cancel any time from My Membership in your dashboard. You get a refund for the days you did not use.

Do I have to share my weight and height?

No. It is optional. You can skip it when you book a session and add it later from your dashboard.

How do promo codes work?

Type the code in the Promo Code box at checkout. New members get a first-order code that is shown on the Plans page.

Can I book a session without a membership?

No. You need an active membership to book sessions with our coaches.
