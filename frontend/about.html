About Connecto Gym

We make joining a gym simple: choose a plan, pick a coach and start training, all online.

Our Story

Connecto Gym started with one idea: signing up for a gym should not mean paper forms, long queues and phone calls. Today members can create an account, compare plans, choose a personal coach, book sessions and pay online in a few minutes.


Our coaches are real professionals with years of experience, and every member can read honest reviews from other members before choosing one.

What We Offer

Flexible Plans

Monthly, 3-month, 6-month and annual memberships to fit your routine and your budget.

Personal Coaches

Strength, yoga, cardio and more. Check ratings and reviews, then book a session time that suits you.

Fair Refunds

Change of plans? Cancel your membership any time and get back the money for the days you did not use.

Special Offers

New members get a welcome discount on their first order, and we share new promo codes all the time.
